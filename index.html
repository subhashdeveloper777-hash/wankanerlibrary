Privacy Policy

Wankaner Library  •  Last updated: September 17, 2026

Wankaner Library ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a free, content-based mobile application providing book summaries, quotes, shayari, poems, and biographies. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

By using Wankaner Library, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1 Data Collection & Accounts

No registration or account creation is required to use Wankaner Library. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media files

You can access all app features without logging in.

However, the app uses third-party services (listed in Section 2) that may automatically collect certain non-personal technical information as described below.

2 Third-Party Services

Wankaner Library uses the following third-party services to deliver content and improve app stability. These services may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

a) Google Firebase (Firebase Core & Cloud Firestore)

  • Purpose: To fetch and display content (book summaries, quotes, shayari, poems, biographies) and to ensure app functionality.
  • Data collected: Firebase may collect a Firebase Installation ID, device identifiers, app usage data, and basic diagnostic information.
  • Data sharing: This data is transmitted to and processed by Google LLC as our service provider.
  • Privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

b) Google Play Services

  • Purpose: To provide core Android services required for the app to function properly.
  • Data collected: Device information, crash logs, and basic performance metrics.
  • Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

c) Firebase Crashlytics (if enabled)

  • Purpose: To detect, diagnose, and fix app crashes and errors.
  • Data collected: Crash logs, device state at the time of crash, and a device identifier.
  • Privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

d) Local Notifications

  • Purpose: To send you daily morning and evening notifications with a random quote or shayari.
  • Data collected: Notification scheduling is handled locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit any personal data for this feature. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

e) Text-to-Speech (TTS)

  • Purpose: To read content aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine.
  • Data collected: TTS processing happens locally on your device using the system TTS engine. No audio or text is sent to our servers.

f) Background Music (Flute & Nature Sounds)

  • Purpose: To play optional background music while you read.
  • Data collected: Audio files are bundled inside the app. No data is collected for this feature.

g) Sharing Feature

  • Purpose: To let you share quotes, shayari, or other content with friends via apps like WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, etc.
  • Data collected: When you tap "Share", the content and an image are generated locally on your device and passed to the app you choose. We do not collect or store what you share. The receiving app's privacy policy applies to the shared content.

3 How We Use Information

Any information collected by the third-party services listed above is used only for the following purposes:

  • To deliver and display content inside the app
  • To ensure the app is stable, secure, and functioning correctly
  • To diagnose crashes and technical issues
  • To improve app performance and user experience
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

4 Data Retention

We do not maintain our own servers that store personal data. Any data collected by Firebase or Google Play Services is retained according to their respective retention policies. Locally stored preferences (such as your dark mode setting, font size, and saved items) remain only on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.

5 Children's Privacy

Wankaner Library is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6 Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over:

  • Notifications: Disable them anytime in your device's Settings → Apps → Wankaner Library → Notifications.
  • Data collected by Firebase: You can reset your advertising/device identifier via your device settings.
  • Local data: Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes all locally saved preferences.

7 Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
